## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the warm, convivial hospitality of a wood-fired pizzeria and traditional dining institution anchored in the central highlands of Huambo. The aesthetic draws on artisanal textures, sun-drenched masonry, aged olive branches, and culinary craftsmanship. It feels established, reliable, and deeply hospitable—neither rustic to the point of disorder nor sleek to the point of sterility.

### Key Tenets
- **Warm & Appetizing:** Visual tones mirror baked dough, slow-cooked pomodoro, toasted herbs, and rich espresso crema.
- **Rooted Hospitality:** Interfaces feel tactile, balanced, and generous, inviting patrons into reservations, takeaway orders, and seasonal specials with seamless clarity.
- **Artisanal Refinement:** Balances classical dining elegance with contemporary operational efficiency, supporting bilingual hospitality (Portuguese primary, English secondary).
- **Design Movement:** Modern Artisanal Tactile—subtle physical cues, warm atmospheric depths, refined serif editorial rhythm, and crisp legible modern UI elements.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the culinary hearth and Huambo's high-plateau soil:

- **Primary (`#B84A2A` - Terracotta Pomodoro):** Represents fired brick, terracotta tiles, and rich reduced tomato sauces. Used for primary CTAs (e.g., "Reservar Mesa", "Pedir Online"), key accents, active states, and focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#5A6844` - Olive Grove):** Evokes fresh oregano, pressed olive oil, and culinary authenticity. Applied to dietary tags (Vegetariano, Forno a Lenha), success states, and secondary visual balances.
- **Tertiary (`#D9933B` - Toasted Crust):** Warm amber used for review stars, chef recommendations, specialty pizza ribbons, and subtle interactive highlights.
- **Neutral Primary (`#241E1C` - Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded, deeply roasted charcoal with a subtle warm undertone. Eliminates harsh jet-black while ensuring high contrast for editorial titles and body typography.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base Background: `#FAF7F2` (Creamy Linen) – Soft, non-glare canvas offering organic warmth.
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Porcelain) – Clean separation for menu lists and reservation forms.
  - Muted Surface: `#F2ECE2` (Warm Biscuit) – Used for table selection grids, bill summaries, and subtle input fills.
  - Border & Dividers: `#E6DED1` (Raw Stone) – Delicate delineation maintaining soft visual flow.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial, heritage character of **Playfair Display** with the clear, high-density legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Reserved for hero narrative titles, section headers (e.g., "Menu de Pizzas Tradicionais", "A Nossa História"), and featured dish names. It evokes timeless culinary hospitality.
- **Functional Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Governs ingredient listings, pricing, operational badges (e.g., "Aberto Agora", "Forno a Lenha"), reservation form inputs, and system notifications.
- **Monetary & Numerical Values:** Menu prices (Kwanza - AOA) must use medium to bold weights of Plus Jakarta Sans with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical alignment down receipt sheets and menu lists.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt baseline rhythm, using a responsive 12-column grid system for large viewports, an 8-column grid for tablets, and a 4-column grid for mobile screens.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (single-column cards, sticky bottom order/booking tray)
  - Tablet: `640px - 1024px` (dual-column menu layouts, condensed header)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column max-width 1240px container, asymmetrical hero banners)
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:**
  - Standard section margins employ generous vertical breathing space (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem on desktop) to evoke a fine, unhurried dining environment.
  - Component internals maintain compact padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure menu readability and compact checkout flows on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through **warm ambient shadows** layered atop soft parchment surfaces, avoiding sterile cold gray drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Menu item entries, table list rows, and inline form controls on `#FFFFFF` surfaces. Demarcated by 1px solid `#E6DED1`.
- **Level 1 (Card & Plate):** Standard menu card, opening hours badge, daily specials.
  - Shadow: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.05), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Popover):** Interactive hovered dish cards, date-picker flyouts, takeaway basket drawer.
  - Shadow: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Table Reservation Engine):** Dialogs, order confirmation bottom sheets, VIP room booking alerts.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(36, 30, 28, 0.16)`.
  - Backdrop: `#241E1C` at 40% opacity with a 4px blur to maintain atmospheric focus.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (`2`)** shape language, reflecting artisanal pottery, pizza peel curves, and table ergonomics.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to standard text inputs, date pickers, dropdown selects, and secondary action buttons.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Used for dish preview cards, photo frames, promo announcement boxes, and reservation calendar sheets.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Utilized for overlay modals, floating checkout panels, and dietary pill groups.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Strictly reserved for status badges (e.g., "Aberto", "Esgotado"), price chips, and floating call buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Reservar Mesa", "Concluir Pedido"):** Terracotta Pomodoro background (`#B84A2A`), crisp white text, `0.5rem` border radius, semi-bold sans-serif. Subtle brightness shift and gentle lift on hover.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Ver Menu Takeaway", "Instruções"):** Warm Biscuit background (`#F2ECE2`), Espresso Charcoal text (`#241E1C`), bordered with `#E6DED1`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, Terracotta text with underline on hover. Used for "Saber mais" or modal cancellation.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators (e.g., "Aberto até às 23:00"):** Olive Grove subtle tint (`#EFF3EB`) with solid `#5A6844` text and a pulsing green focal dot.
- **Menu Categorization Chips:** Pill-shaped selector tags (e.g., "Pizzas no Forno", "Massas Frescas", "Bebidas", "Sobremesas"). Inactive: transparent with 1px border. Active: filled with `#241E1C` and white text.

### Cards (Menu & Food)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 warm ambient shadows.
- Dish photo retains a 4:3 or 1:1 aspect ratio with rounded corners (`1rem`).
- Dish title styled in Playfair Display (`title-lg` or `headline-sm`), with ingredients displayed in muted body text (`#665D59`).
- Price anchored prominently in bottom right using Plus Jakarta Sans bold tabular numbers.

### Form Inputs & Reservation Fields
- Container: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px solid `#E6DED1`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus State: Border shifts to Terracotta (`#B84A2A`) with a soft matching ambient glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 74, 42, 0.15)`).
- Error State: Muted crimson border with inline error helper text.

### Table Reservation Selector
- Interactive floor/seat/date matrix with warm visual cues:
  - Available: Biscuit background (`#F2ECE2`), hoverable.
  - Selected: Filled Terracotta Pomodoro (`#B84A2A`) with white iconography.
  - Reserved/Unavailable: Light line hatch with opacity `0.4`.
